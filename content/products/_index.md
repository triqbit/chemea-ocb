## Premium Carbon Black Grades

Chemea™ Oman Carbon Black (OCB) supplies ASTM‑aligned carbon black grades through qualified third‑party producers for tire, rubber, plastic, and industrial applications. Final properties and limits for each grade are always confirmed via producer Certificates of Analysis (CoA).

### Grade Portfolio

**Reinforcing Grades (Tire & Mechanical Rubber)**  
N‑220 · N‑234 · N‑326 · N‑330 · N‑339 · N‑347 · N‑375  
These grades are typically used in tire treads and high‑performance rubber compounds where reinforcement, abrasion resistance, and modulus are critical.

**Semi‑Reinforcing Grades**  
N‑550 · N‑660  
Semi‑reinforcing blacks are widely used in sidewalls, inner liners, tubes, profiles, and general‑purpose rubber where a balance of processability and physical properties is required.

**Specialty / Application‑Specific Grades**  
N‑550 Chemea™ (S‑SO) · N‑330 Chemea™ (V‑3) · P‑Type ICJ‑P‑8201  
These designations represent application‑oriented supply concepts based on producer materials and documentation. The underlying ASTM grade, producer, and exact specification are confirmed case by case.

### Standards Overview

ASTM International standards dominate classification and testing of rubber‑grade carbon blacks worldwide, and many regional or national standards adopt or reference these methods. For carbon black content in polyolefin pipes and fittings, **ASTM D1603** and **ISO 6964** both rely on thermal decomposition (calcination / pyrolysis), while ISO 6964 is tailored specifically to pipe and fitting products.

### Shelf Life and Storage

ASTM D8043 indicates that carbon black has an **indefinite shelf life** when stored to protect it from liquid water and high humidity; key physical characteristics such as particle size and structure are not expected to degrade with time. Moisture and iodine number may change over time, so stored material should be evaluated before use.

Store carbon black in a dry, covered, well‑ventilated area, away from liquid water, high humidity, and incompatible materials to maintain handling and testing consistency.
