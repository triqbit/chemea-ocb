### Application Guides

#### Carbon Black for Tire Manufacturing
- Grade selection: N-220, N-330, N-339 for tread; N-550, N-660 for sidewall
- Typical loadings: 35–50 phr (parts per hundred rubber)
- Performance benefits: Reinforcement, wear resistance, heat dissipation
- Testing: Rolling resistance (ASTM F880), abrasion (ASTM D1630), tensile properties (ASTM D412)

#### Carbon Black for Rubber Goods
- Common grades: N-550, N-660 for hoses, belts, seals
- Loadings: 20–40 phr depending on performance requirements
- Applications: Engine hoses, V-belts, gaskets, diaphragms, vibration isolators
- Key properties: Tensile strength, tear resistance, resilience

#### Carbon Black for Plastics & Compounds
- Typical grades: N-550, N-660 for general use; P-Type ICJ-P-8201 for UV-critical applications
- Loadings: 2–5 phr for pigmentation, 5–15 phr for UV protection
- Applications: Pipes (pressure & gravity), geomembranes, films, compounds
- Standards: ISO 6964 (pipe), ASTM G154 (UV resistance)

#### Carbon Black for Conductive & Antistatic Applications
- Specialized grades with optimized surface chemistry
- Typical loadings: 10–30 phr
- Testing: Volume resistivity (ASTM D991), surface resistivity (EOS/ESD S11.11)

### Safety Data Sheets (SDS) & Certificates of Analysis (CoA)

All SDS and batch CoA are available upon request and sourced directly from producers. Documents include:

- **SDS Components**:
  - Hazard classification and signal words
  - GHS pictograms and hazard statements
  - First aid, handling, storage guidance
  - Exposure controls and PPE requirements
  - Physical and chemical properties
  - Ecological and disposal information

- **CoA Components**:
  - Batch number and date of manufacture
  - ASTM test results (iodine, DBP, surface area, etc.)
  - Pass/fail against specification limits
  - Producer contact information

### Glossary of Key Terms

**ASTM**: American Society for Testing and Materials; develops consensus-based standards

**CoA**: Certificate of Analysis; batch test results from the producer

**DBP**: Dibutyl Phthalate; measures oil absorption and carbon black structure

**Iodine Number**: Indicator of surface area; higher = finer particles, higher reinforcement

**N-Series**: ASTM classification for rubber-grade furnace blacks (N-110 through N-990)

**pH**: Measure of surface chemistry acidity/alkalinity; affects compound interactions

**SDS**: Safety Data Sheet; hazard, handling, and emergency information

**Tint Strength**: Measure of pigmentation efficiency and jetness (color intensity)

### Regulatory & Compliance Information

#### REACH (EU)
Carbon black is registered under REACH (Registration, Evaluation, Authorization, and Restriction of Chemicals). Producer REACH dossiers and Safety Data Sheets are available upon request.

#### ROHS (EU)
Carbon black is not restricted under ROHS 2 (Restrictions of Hazardous Substances Directive).

#### China Chemical Inventory (CCI)
Carbon black is listed on China's chemical inventory. Producer documentation confirms registration status.

#### India Chemical Inventory
Carbon black is listed on India's Inventory of Hazardous Chemical Substances and Preparation.

#### TSCA (USA)
Carbon black is listed on the TSCA Inventory (Toxic Substances Control Act).

**Verify regulatory status**: Contact **OCB@chemea.com** with destination market for specific compliance documentation.

### Logistics & Incoterms Reference

**Common Incoterms Explained**:
- **EXW** (Ex Works): Seller responsibility ends at factory gate
- **FOB** (Free on Board): Seller pays freight to port; buyer from port onward
- **CFR** (Cost and Freight): Seller pays freight but not insurance
- **CIF** (Cost, Insurance, and Freight): Seller pays all costs including insurance
- **DAP** (Delivered at Place): Seller delivers to buyer's location

**Packaging for Logistics**:
- Paper bags (21–28 kg): Manual handling, small silos
- Jumbo bags (1000–1200 kg): Automated handling, efficient storage
- Pallet configurations: 20–25 bags per pallet, 400–600 kg per pallet
