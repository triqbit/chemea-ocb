## Governance, Privacy & Compliance

Chemea™ Oman Carbon Black (OCB) operates with **data minimization at its core**, collecting only business contact information and technical project details necessary to prepare accurate quotations, documentation, and logistics coordination.

### Data Minimization

We do not collect unnecessary personal information. Website forms request only:

- Company name, contact person, and email / phone
- Technical application details (grade, quantity, destination, timeline)
- Preferred packaging and Incoterms

This information is used solely for quotation preparation and RFQ fulfillment.

### Analytics & Cookies

Our site uses **cookie‑light or cookie‑free analytics** (if any), configured to measure performance without building user behavior profiles. We do not:

- Enable third‑party tracking cookies
- Share data with ad networks
- Build retargeting audiences

If anonymized, aggregated analytics are used, they operate under strict data protection and with strong IP anonymization.

### Data Subject Rights

Individuals may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data stored by Chemea™ OCB by contacting:

**Email**: OCB@chemea.com  
**Subject**: "Data Subject Request"

Requests are handled in accordance with applicable data protection standards and B2B record‑keeping requirements. **Standard response time: 30 days**.

### Data Security

All contact information and quotation details are kept confidential and secured using industry‑standard encryption in transit and at rest. Access is limited to authorized personnel only for the purposes stated above.

### Data Retention

- **RFQ/contact data**: Retained for 2 years for business record purposes; can be deleted on request
- **Request deletion anytime**: Contact OCB@chemea.com with subject "Delete my data"
- **Response time**: 14 days

### Compliance Disclaimers

**Product Data**: All technical specifications, SDS, CoA, and regulatory statements originate from third‑party producers and are provided as‑is; customers are responsible for confirming suitability and compliance.

**Logistics & Documentation**: Incoterms, packaging, and documentation scope are subject to producer availability and regulatory requirements in destination markets; final arrangements are confirmed at order placement.

### Updates to This Policy

Chemea™ OCB may update this privacy and governance policy as regulations evolve or operational practices change. Material changes will be communicated via this page and email notification to known contacts where applicable.

For questions or concerns, contact **OCB@chemea.com**.
