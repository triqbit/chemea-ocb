## Careers at Chemea™ OCB

Chemea™ Oman Carbon Black (OCB) is growing. We're looking for talented professionals who are passionate about supply chain excellence, technical precision, and customer success. Join us in building the future of carbon black trading.

### Why Work With Us?

- **Growth mindset**: Work in a fast-growing, dynamic trading organization
- **Global impact**: Serve customers across 50+ countries
- **Expertise**: Partner with industry leaders and top-tier producers worldwide
- **Oman advantage**: Positioned at the crossroads of Asia, Africa, and Europe
- **Innovation**: Drive improvements in documentation, logistics, and supply coordination
- **Competitive compensation**: Attractive package with performance incentives

### Open Positions

#### Supply Chain Coordinator
**Location**: Muscat, Oman  
**Experience**: 2–3 years in logistics, procurement, or supply chain  

**Key responsibilities**:
- Manage RFQs and quotation process
- Coordinate shipments and documentation
- Liaise with producers and logistics partners
- Track KPIs for lead times and cost efficiency

**Requirements**:
- Bachelor's degree in Supply Chain, Business, or related field
- Fluency in English (Arabic a plus)
- Experience with Incoterms and customs procedures
- Attention to detail and excellent communication

---

#### Technical Sales Representative
**Location**: Muscat, Oman (Remote options available)  
**Experience**: 3–5 years in B2B sales or technical support  

**Key responsibilities**:
- Respond to customer RFQs with technical guidance
- Explain ASTM standards, grades, and applications
- Build long-term customer relationships
- Identify upsell and expansion opportunities

**Requirements**:
- Bachelor's degree in Chemistry, Engineering, Materials Science, or Business
- Deep understanding of carbon black applications (tire, rubber, plastics)
- Strong communication and problem-solving skills
- CRM experience (Salesforce, HubSpot, or similar)

---

#### Quality & Compliance Officer
**Location**: Muscat, Oman  
**Experience**: 2–4 years in quality assurance or regulatory compliance  

**Key responsibilities**:
- Review and manage SDS, CoA, and regulatory documentation
- Ensure compliance with ASTM, ISO, and regional standards
- Coordinate with producers on batch testing and certification
- Maintain documentation systems and audit trails

**Requirements**:
- Bachelor's degree in Chemistry, Engineering, or Compliance
- Knowledge of ASTM testing methods and standards
- Experience with regulatory frameworks (REACH, ROHS, etc.)
- Strong organizational and analytical skills

---

#### Operations Manager
**Location**: Muscat, Oman  
**Experience**: 5–7 years in operations or business management  

**Key responsibilities**:
- Oversee daily operations and team coordination
- Manage budgets, KPIs, and performance metrics
- Drive process improvements and efficiency gains
- Lead cross-functional projects with producers and partners

**Requirements**:
- Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Operations, or related field
- Proven track record in P&L management
- Leadership and team-building skills
- Experience with ERP or operational management systems
