## Industries Served

Chemea™ Oman Carbon Black (OCB) delivers application‑specific carbon black grades from qualified producers to support tires, rubber goods, plastics, and broader industrial uses. Different ASTM grades are selected to balance reinforcement, processing, and durability per application.

### Tire Industry

High‑reinforcement grades are used in treads, carcasses, and sidewalls to enhance abrasion resistance, traction, fatigue life, and overall tire durability. Specific ASTM N‑series grades are chosen for tread versus carcass and sidewall compounds to tune rolling resistance, heat build‑up, and cut‑chip performance.

**Common grades**: N‑220, N‑330, N‑339

### Rubber Goods

Carbon black strengthens hoses, belts, seals, gaskets, diaphragms, and molded components, improving tensile strength, tear resistance, and wear while supporting processability in extrusion and molding. Semi‑reinforcing grades such as N‑550 and N‑660 are common where a balance of stiffness, elasticity, and smooth surface finish is required.

**Common grades**: N‑550, N‑660

### Plastics & Compounds

In plastics and compounds, carbon black provides deep pigmentation and long‑term UV protection for pipes, cables, geomembranes, films, and technical parts. Specialized P‑type and high‑purity grades are often used in pressure pipes and geomembranes to meet UV durability and regulatory requirements over decades of outdoor exposure.

**Specialty grades**: P‑Type ICJ‑P‑8201, N‑330 Chemea™ (V‑3)

### Industrial & Functional Applications

Carbon black is also used in cable jacketing, conductive or antistatic plastics, profiles, and other industrial components where electrical properties and consistent performance are critical.

**Applications**: Wire & cable, mining equipment, electronics housings
