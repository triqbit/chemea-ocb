## About Chemea™ Oman Carbon Black (OCB)

Headquartered in Muscat, Oman, Chemea™ Oman Carbon Black (OCB) is a specialized trading organization serving global industrial markets with premium ASTM‑aligned carbon black grades sourced from qualified third‑party producers. Oman's position as an emerging logistics hub between Asia, Africa, and Europe supports efficient routing and resilient supply options for customers.

### Mission & Strengths

- Resilient supply coordination that reduces procurement complexity and mitigates single‑source risk.
- Diversified sourcing from multiple qualified global systems, aligned with ASTM testing and documentation practices.
- Sustainability focus that prioritizes efficient logistics routes and lower‑impact transport choices where feasible.
- Rapid, transparent responses with clear documentation pathways, including SDS and batch CoA issued by producers upon request.

OCB does not manufacture carbon black—all specifications and documentation originate from the respective producers, and customers remain responsible for confirming suitability and regulatory compliance in their own applications.

### Why a Trading Organization?

Unlike manufacturers tied to single plants and supply sources, Chemea™ OCB delivers:

| Advantage | How It Works | Benefit |
|-----------|------------|---------|
| **Diversified Sourcing** | Access to 10+ qualified producer systems globally | Eliminates single-source risk; resilient supply even during disruptions |
| **Transparent Documentation** | CoA and SDS sourced directly from actual producers | No middleman; customers see real test data, not rebranded specs |
| **Optimized Logistics** | Oman hub positioned between Asia, Africa, Europe | Shorter lead times, flexible routing, reduced total landed cost |
| **Flexible Scale** | 21kg bags to 1200kg jumbo shipments, custom packaging | No minimum order penalties; trial quantities to bulk contracts |
| **Commercial Clarity** | Clear Incoterms, consistent pricing, rapid RFQ responses | Faster procurement cycles; budget certainty |

**The OCB Advantage**: We handle the complexity—sourcing, documentation, logistics coordination—so your team focuses on formulation and quality assurance, not supplier relationship management.
