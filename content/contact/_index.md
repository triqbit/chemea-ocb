### What to Include in Your RFQ

For the fastest evaluation, please share:

- **Application**: Tire, rubber goods, plastics, or industrial
- **Grade**: Specific ASTM grade (e.g., N‑330, N‑550) or reference standard if known
- **Quantity**: Volume per shipment and expected frequency
- **Packaging**: Bag size, jumbo bags, custom requirements
- **Destination**: Country, port, or delivery address
- **Incoterms**: Your preferred delivery terms (EXW, FOB, CFR, CIF, DAP)
- **Timeline**: Target delivery date or flexibility window
- **Documentation**: SDS, CoA, regulatory statements, or other compliance needs

### Typical RFQ Process

1. **Submit RFQ** with details above
2. **Receive quotation** within 24–48 hours with pricing, timeline, and available documentation
3. **Confirm specs** and delivery terms
4. **Receive documentation** (SDS, CoA) from producer
5. **Arrange shipment** with agreed packaging and Incoterms
6. **Track delivery** through logistics partner
7. **Verify receipt** and confirm batch documentation
