## Logistics & Packaging Coordination

Chemea™ Oman Carbon Black (OCB) coordinates packaging and shipment terms with producers and logistics partners so deliveries align with your plant handling, storage, and scheduling needs. Commercial carbon black is typically shipped as a non‑hazardous material under major transport regulations, but still requires appropriate packaging and handling controls.

### Packaging Options

- **Paper bags: ~21–28 kg**  
  Common for bagged deliveries and manual or semi‑automated feeding into mixers and small silos.

- **Jumbo / FIBC bags: ~1000–1200 kg**  
  One‑ton big bags for bulk handling, often with top filling spouts and bottom discharge for use with bag‑dischargers and silo systems.

- **Custom options**  
  Custom labelling, palletization, stretch‑wrapping, and liner / bag variants are possible depending on the producer and packer.

Final packaging format, dimensions, and loading patterns are confirmed at quotation stage for each lane and producer.

### Delivery Terms

Quotations can be offered under widely used **Incoterms 2020** such as **EXW, FOB, CFR, CIF, and DAP**, depending on route, modal mix, and buyer preference.

Clarifying your preferred Incoterm early helps define who manages inland transport, export formalities, main carriage, insurance, and last‑mile delivery, and improves landed‑cost and risk planning.

### Recommended RFQ Details

To enable accurate routing, pricing, and lead‑time proposals, include:

- Final destination (country, city, port / ICD or delivery address)
- Preferred Incoterms and any routing or carrier constraints
- Packaging preference (bag size, jumbo / FIBC, palletization, special labelling)
- Quantity per shipment and expected frequency (spot vs recurring)
- Required timeline or delivery window and any flexibility
- Documentation needs (SDS, CoA, regulatory statements, specific standards to reference)

Safe handling, transport, and storage guidance for each shipment is provided via the producer's SDS and recognized industry guidelines for carbon black transport and big‑bag handling.
