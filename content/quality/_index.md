## Quality & Compliance Support

Chemea™ Oman Carbon Black (OCB) supports QA and regulatory teams by coordinating access to producer‑verified documentation and clearly mapped test methods for supplied carbon black grades. All technical data, limits, and regulatory statements originate from the respective manufacturers.

### Quality Evaluation

Grades are evaluated using standardized ASTM test methods commonly applied to rubber‑grade carbon black, such as iodine number, DBP absorption, surface area, and pellet properties, as reflected in producer data sheets and CoA.

For each shipment, a batch Certificate of Analysis (CoA) is available upon request, summarizing key parameters and confirming that the product meets the producer's specification for that grade.

### Documentation

- Safety Data Sheets (SDS) for the relevant carbon black grades, covering health, safety, and environmental guidance.
- Certificates of Analysis (CoA) issued by producers, detailing measured properties for each batch.
- Regulatory and compliance data (where available), such as product regulatory status, inventory listings, and regional declarations supplied by manufacturers.

Availability and scope of documentation depend on the specific producer and grade identified in the RFQ.

### Customer Responsibilities

Each customer remains responsible for:

- Verifying that supplied grades and specifications are suitable for their own formulations, processes, and end‑use markets using the SDS, CoA, and regulatory information.
- Ensuring regulatory compliance, labeling, and product stewardship in all jurisdictions where finished products are manufactured or sold.
- Implementing safe handling, storage, exposure controls, and housekeeping practices in line with the SDS and applicable local regulations.

### Governance, Privacy & Data Requests

Chemea™ Oman Carbon Black (OCB) operates with **data minimization at its core**, collecting only business contact information and technical project details necessary to prepare accurate quotations, documentation, and logistics coordination.

Where analytics are used, they are configured to operate with minimal identifiers and strong aggregation. We do not enable third‑party tracking cookies, share data with ad networks, or build retargeting audiences.

Data subjects can request access, correction, or deletion of their personal data by contacting **OCB@chemea.com**, and such requests are handled in line with applicable data protection standards and B2B record‑keeping obligations. Standard response time: 30 days.
